# Bot Bridge – brand

* **Mark:** two nodes joined by a bridge arc (your assistant ⇄ your browser), white on a rounded square.
* **Accent:** Indigo `#4F46E5` (hover/dark `#4338CA`, tint `#EEF2FF`). Ink `#0F172A`, muted `#64748B`, surface `#F8FAFC`, border `#E2E8F0`.
* **Status colours:** connected `#4F46E5` badge "ON"; error `#DC2626` badge "!"; success text `#047857` on `#ECFDF5`.
* **Type:** Inter (fallback: system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif). Headlines 700, body 400/500.
* **Files:** `icon.svg` (master, 128 grid), `icon-16.svg` (pixel-tuned 16 px), `store-icon.svg` (96 px artwork + 16 px padding).
  Render with `python3 render_all.py` → `extension/icons/icon{16,32,48,128}.png`, `store/images/store-icon-128.png`.
