Your AI assistant,
in your own Chrome.

Bot Bridge lets your own AI assistant open tabs, read pages, click, type, and place files you name into your browser, through a small bridge app on your computer. The extension talks only to that bridge. Your assistant may process the results off this device.

Download the bridge View on GitHub Chrome Web Store: coming soon
1
Run the bridge

Unzip bridge-app.zip and start python3 bridge.py serve. It shows the port. Show the token with bridge.py token in a terminal you control.

2
Add the extension

Install Bot Bridge in Chrome, agree to the notice, paste port and token. The icon shows ON.

3
Ask your assistant

Your assistant sends commands like get_page, click or type – the extension does the rest.

Direct link is local. The assistant may not be.

The extension only talks to the bridge on 127.0.0.1. It does not send data to the developer. There is no tracking. Your chosen assistant receives the results and may process them off this device. Limited Use: Bot Bridge uses this data only so that assistant can operate your browser, the extension's single purpose. It is not sold or used for advertising or credit decisions.
